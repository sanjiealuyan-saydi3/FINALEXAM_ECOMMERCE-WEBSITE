THE IDEA BEHIND FORME

Good style shouldn't be hard work.

We built the FORME concept around one simple idea: a jacket is often the piece that makes an outfit feel finished. It should be easy to wear, easy to style, and comfortable enough to reach for again and again.

Instead of chasing every trend, our fictional collection focuses on familiar silhouettes, neutral colors, and practical details. Think of a lightweight zip jacket for everyday errands, a coach jacket for relaxed streetwear, or a utility layer for a more structured look.

This website is a school e-commerce project. FORME is not presented as an operating retailer; all product names, descriptions, prices, and policies are sample content created for demonstration.
